# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and assume good intent while prioritizing safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, publishing private information, or sustained disruption. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently exclude participants when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Reports will be handled confidentially and fairly.
